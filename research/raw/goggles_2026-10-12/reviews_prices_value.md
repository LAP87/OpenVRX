## 1. What do the main reviewers say about each candidate (HDMI/DP input behaviour, latency, picture)?

### Takeaway
For FPV goggles the HDMI-input record is reasonably documented: HDZero claims about 1 ms through its HDMI path, Walksnail Goggles X gained explicit 720p50/1080p50 HDMI-input support in firmware 39.44.18 (4 Jun 2026), while Skyzone Cobra X-class goggles are the slow case (37 ms HDMI-only measured on a V2; 76-80 ms in system tests). For XR glasses no reviewer measured display latency or documented 720p / 50 Hz behaviour; the one hands-on FPV test found (IntoFPV, Aug-Sep 2026) shows VITURE Pro 2 behind a dock and HDMI-to-DP converter accepting 1080p60/120 but no 720p mode at all.

### Inferences
- The most receiver-friendly FPV HDMI inputs are HDZero (claimed 1 ms, firmware tracks CEA-861 timing, ITE IT66021 EDID/timing fixes still shipping) and, since 4 Jun 2026, Walksnail Goggles X (explicit 720p50/1080p50 plus 720p/1080p switching). The slowest and most buffered is the Skyzone Cobra X path (FPGA rotation; 37 ms HDMI-only on a V2, 76-80 ms in system tests). Together they bracket what an HDMI-output receiver will meet in goggles: roughly 1 ms to 40+ ms from the HDMI path alone.
- All published FPV HDMI latencies are chain numbers measured with phone/GoPro slow motion (+/- 4 ms or worse) on 2022-2023 firmware; only one HDMI-input-only measurement exists (Cobra X V2, 37 ms). They should not be quoted as the current HDMI-input latency of the named goggles.
- Zero-buffer HDMI paths (HDZero) are probably the least tolerant of a slightly varying frame length / 59.94 Hz drift, while scaler or frame-buffer paths (Skyzone, XR glasses behind DP converters) will tolerate it at the cost of latency. This is my inference from the claimed 1 ms path and the continuing timing/EDID fixes; no source tests variable frame length.
- XR glasses are the high-risk category for a 720p-only output: the VITURE Pro 2 chain advertises no 720p EDID and the one XREAL EDID excerpt found lists only 1080p60/90/120. A receiver aimed at XR glasses should read the sink EDID and offer a 1080p60 (or 1080p50/120) fallback or a built-in upscaler; otherwise 720p-only output may give no picture.
- Picture classes: 1080p OLED goggles (Goggles X, SKY04X Pro, HDO+) are sharper than 720p LCD box goggles (Cobra X, BoxPro) when fed 1080p, but for a 720p60 source a 720p-native panel (BoxPro, Cobra X, SKY04O Pro) avoids scaler artefacts. Birdbath/prism XR glasses add edge blur and very low transparency (Guttag), so they are viewing screens, not see-through displays.
